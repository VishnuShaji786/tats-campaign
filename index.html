Solo Creator Operating System — The Pillar → Atom Model
1 PILLAR/WEEK
Odd weeks: 1 Reel is the week's anchor. Even weeks: 1 Podcast episode. Record once. Everything else derives from it. You never start from a blank page again.
ATOMS THAT FLOW FROM IT
2 Feed posts (key insight + student angle) · 4 Story slides (BTS, teaser, quote, poll) · 1 YT Short clip · LinkedIn text reframed for Chintu's voice. One idea, six pieces.
WHY 114 NOT 180
Buffer ships 3–4 pieces/week with 1 writer. HubSpot's growth content team is 2 people. 180 required a 3–4 person crew. 114 you can ship every week without burning out — consistency always beats volume.

Why These Times

Aviation cadets and parents are early risers. Peak engagement: 7–8am (morning check) and 7–9pm (evening wind-down). LinkedIn is weekday mornings only. YouTube is weekend viewing. Stories 4 days/week — every other day — maintains top-of-feed presence without daily production pressure.

⚠ Timing Non-Negotiables
Never miss 2 story days in a row

Stories are Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun. Missing consecutive days breaks the trust loop. Stories build the relationship that turns passive followers into DMs.

Podcast uploaded by 4pm Saturday

YouTube needs 1–2hrs to process before your 6pm publish. Never upload and publish simultaneously. Bi-weekly means more prep time — use it.

Reply to DMs within 1hr

DM-to-call conversion drops 60% if first reply takes over 3 hours. This is your highest-leverage 60 minutes. No content task outranks an unread DM.
