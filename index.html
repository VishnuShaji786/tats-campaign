⚠ Timing Non-Negotiables
Never go dark on Stories

Missing 2 consecutive story days kills retention. Stories build the trust that turns passive followers into DMs.

Podcast uploaded by 4pm Saturday

YouTube needs 1–2hrs to process before your 6pm publish. Never upload and publish simultaneously.

Reply to DMs within 1hr

DM-to-call conversion drops 60% if first reply takes over 3 hours. This is your highest-leverage 60 minutes.
